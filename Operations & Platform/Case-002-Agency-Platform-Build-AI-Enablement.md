# AI Enablement +  Operations Platform Consolidation


## Context

Agency Internal Platform Build

Consolidated eight disconnected operational tools into one internal operations platform for a 25+ person agency.

The agency had grown into a multi-tool setup where delivery, QA, billing, reporting, and documentation lived across multiple systems.

The objective was to consolidate operations into one operational platform without disrupting delivery.

## The problem ("the chaos")

- Data silos across teams
- Workflow friction caused by disconnected systems
- Billing and reporting risk
- Inconsistent processes across a growing agency
- Multiple systems for CRM, QA, finance, reporting, freelancers, and documentation

## Goal

Build an operational platform capable of answering:

- What is the current delivery status?
- What is the source of truth for finance?
- Where is operational knowledge stored?
- How can context switching be reduced?

---

## Solution

Created one internal operational platform that consolidated workflows and business data.

**Key challenge**

Consolidating finance and billing workflows without revenue leakage while maintaining delivery continuity.

## My approach
