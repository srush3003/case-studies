## Context

The agency had grown rapidly.

Resource planning remained reactive.

The objective was to introduce real-time resource planning without disrupting delivery.

---

## The problem ("the chaos")

- No visibility into resource allocations
- Designers exceeding planned utilization
- Slack used for availability management
- Founder manually resolving allocations

---

## Goal

Create a system answering:

- Who is available?
- What is already committed?
- What requires replanning?

---

## Solution

Selected Productive for resource planning and availability management.

### Key challenge

Recreate every ClickUp workflow with zero downtime.

---

## My approach

1. Mapped 27 workflows
2. Migrated 3,000+ tasks and 20+ projects
3. Documented SOPs
4. Trained 32 team members
5. Limited founder involvement to strategic approvals

---

## Impact

### Capacity Planning

- Visibility increased to 100%
- Overbooking reduced to 3%

### Time

- Founder time reduced from 8 hours/week to approximately 30 minutes/week

### Adoption

- 92% adoption
- Faster onboarding through documentation
